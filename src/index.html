    Rules
    • Goal. Eliminate all four enemy heroes. Beasts and creeps don't count.
    • One action per turn: move one unit, or use an active skill. Moving onto an enemy eliminates it.
    • Roles. Roam (Ge'nga, Aqna, Hexor) move up to 2 squares straight; Hexor's L jump counts as his 2 steps. Assassin (Hasim) steps 1 square in any direction, plus his Backstab jump. Support (Plantae) steps 1 but reaches 3 with its skill. Magumba slides up to 2 diagonally.
    • Terrain (pillars, trees, ruins, rocks, ice) blocks movement. Only Hasim's Backstab and Hexor's jump go over it; nobody lands on it.
    • Skill tokens pay for every skill, passive or active. Both sides start with 4 (the max) and they don't refill each turn: you only earn +1 for killing an enemy creep or getting your creep to the far rank. Costs follow impact (Hasim's Backstab and Ge'nga's beast moves are free): ice 1, Empty Grave 2, Storm Hammer 2, summoning the beast 3, Aggressive Root 3 (interrupt 3).
    • Hasim · Backstab (signature move, free). Jumps in any of the 8 directions over the adjacent unit or terrain, landing 2 squares away. A jumped enemy and an enemy on the landing square are both eliminated. Gold diamonds mark jumps.
    • Ge'nga · Wild Company. Right after he acts his beast may also move (free), or skip. Active (3 tokens): once the beast has fallen, summon it again on an empty square next to him; this uses your turn. It flees if he falls.
    • Magumba · Empty Grave (2 tokens, range 3): an ally can't be captured, rooted, pushed or harmed until your next turn. Clears Root.
    • Plantae · Aggressive Root (3 tokens, range 3, cooldown 3): on your turn, roots an enemy for its next turn. On the enemy's turn, when one of their units within 3 acts, you may interrupt for 3 tokens: the action is negated and their turn ends. The catch (the hole): Plantae roots itself and can't act on your next turn. Either use puts it on cooldown for 3 of your turns.
    • Hexor · Storm Hammer (2 tokens). After he captures, you may pay to slam: every unit around him (allies too) is pushed 1 square away, chaining. An enemy pushed into terrain or off the board is eliminated; an ally that would be pushed there stays put instead.
    • Aqna · Icy Step (1 token). After she eliminates an enemy, she may raise an ice block within 3 squares. Max 10; after that her oldest block moves.
    • Creeps. Each side starts with 4 pawns on the flank lanes (files a, b, g, h), facing the enemy's creeps. Like chess pawns, you move them yourself (it uses your turn): 1 square forward onto an empty square, or capture an enemy diagonally in front. Map terrain sits only in the four centre files, so it never blocks a lane. Eliminating an enemy creep gives +1 token; a creep that reaches the far rank breaks through for +1 token. Creeps don't count for victory. Hexor can push them, Hasim can jump them, Aqna's kills on them raise ice.
    • Distances count diagonals as 1, like a chess king's steps: "within 3" means the 7×7 square around the hero. Select Plantae to see its reach tinted green. Each hero's movement is shown on its card.
    • Controls. Click a hero, then a marked square. Q or the skill button arms an active skill. Esc cancels.
