    Rules
    • Goal. Eliminate all three enemy heroes. No king, no check.
    • Opponent. By default you play Ivory against a bot playing Ember. Easy makes loose moves, Normal looks one exchange ahead, Hard looks a move deeper. Undo takes back your last move and the bot's reply.
    • One action per turn: move one hero, or use one ability.
    • Movement. Assassin moves like a knight and jumps. Mage and Guardian slide like bishop and rook and are blocked by any unit.
    • Capture. Moving onto an enemy's square eliminates it, exactly like chess. Sliders can only capture the first unit in their line. A red square marks a capture.
    • Skills are scarce. Default: each hero's two abilities can be used once per match. The Skills menu can switch to a shared pool of 1 or 3 uses per player; it applies from the next new match.
    • Setup is point-symmetric (180° rotation), so both Mages stand on the same square colour and can reach each other.
    • Blink Strike. Land on an empty square a knight-move away that touches the target (diagonals count), then eliminate it. Pick the target, then the landing square if there is a choice.
    • Arcane Bolt hits the first unit on a diagonal at any range. If that unit is an ally, the bolt can't fire that way.
    • Ice Prison (range 3) freezes an enemy for its owner's next turn. Frozen heroes can still be swapped, shielded, pushed or eliminated.
    • Shield (range 2, another ally only, no stacking) lasts until it blocks one elimination: a capture, Blink Strike, Arcane Bolt, or a push off the board. It ignores freezes and ordinary pushes. A blocked capture leaves the attacker where it was; a blocked Blink still moves the Assassin; a blocked edge push leaves the target in place.
    • Knockback pushes an adjacent enemy (diagonals count) one square straight away. Off the board eliminates; an occupied square means it can't be used.
    • Shadow Swap trades places with an ally within 3 squares.
    • A side with no legal action passes automatically. Invalid actions never use up a turn.
    • Controls. Click a hero, then a dot to move or a red square to capture. Q / W or the buttons arm an ability, then click a highlighted target. Esc cancels.
